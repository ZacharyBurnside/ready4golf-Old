How it works

From group chat chaos to a tee time in three taps.

No spreadsheets, no scrolling back through fifty messages.

1

Invite your group

Send one link. Friends tap it, the app opens, and they’re in. Your group stays in sync across everyone’s phones.

2

Tap when you’re open

Early, late morning, midday or twilight. Mark the blocks that work for the next two weeks.

3

See the overlap

The best window sits at the top, with who’s in and who you’re still waiting on. Copy it to the group chat and book it.

Silence isn’t a no

Coarse blocks, not exact times

Plan ahead

The next two weeks at a glance.

A grid shows how many players are free in every block, so the good days jump out before you start texting.

  • Counts for early, late morning, midday and twilight
  • Windows with enough players are highlighted
  • See who still hasn’t answered

Fast to fill in

Set your week in seconds.

Press and hold a day to mark yourself open all day, repeat every Saturday, or save a standing default.

  • “I’m open all day” in one tap
  • Repeat a day every week
  • Mark days you’re out so no one waits on you

Free for a foursome. Pro for the whole crew.

Free groups hold up to 4 players, and you can organize 1 group. Pro removes both limits. The organizer subscribes and everyone in their group gets Pro.

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or annual). Manage or cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.
